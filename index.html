Impact of U.S. Fed Rate Regimes on Market Returns

Business Problem

How do changes in U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate regimes influence Indian
(NIFTY) and global (S&P 500) market returns and volatility patterns?

Methodology

  • Collected multi-market historical datasets
  • Classified interest rate regimes (Low / Medium / High)
  • Performed correlation & volatility analysis
  • Compared pre and post rate-hike market reactions
  • Highlighted crisis periods (2008 & COVID-19)

Key Insights

Returns were strongest during low-rate environments, while volatility
increased during high-rate regimes.
